# Maximus Members: administrator guide

For the Owner, Managers and Staff of Maximus Collectibles. No technical knowledge needed.

Staff app: **members.maximusnj.com/staff** · Member page: **members.maximusnj.com/my**

## Who can do what

| | Owner | Manager | Staff |
|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| Register and edit customers, check-in kiosk, record purchases, refunds and trades | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ |
| Enter members in a raffle in store, mark winners collected/expired/declined, Next in line | ✔ | ✔ (own stores) | ✔ (own stores) |
| Dashboard (members, visits, recent activity) | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ (no money figures) |
| Reports (tiers, sales, raffles) | ✔ | ✔ (own stores) | ✖ |
| Create raffles, run the draw, press Notify, export the list | ✔ | ✔ (own-store raffles) | ✖ |
| All-stores raffle | ✔ | ✖ | ✖ |
| Make members active/inactive, reset a PIN, link an online login | ✔ | ✔ | ✖ |
| Void a transaction, merge duplicates, set a tier by hand | ✔ | ✖ | ✖ |
| Tier settings, Monthly update, Stores, Employees, Audit log | ✔ | (Employees: invite Staff) | ✖ |
| Download or erase a member's data | ✔ | ✖ | ✖ |

## Every day

**Sign in** at /staff, then choose your store (top bar). On a shared computer, always press *Sign out*.

**Register a new customer:** Customers → *Register customer*. Fill in the name and mobile phone. The app warns if the person may already be a member. A member number (MAX…) is created for you. Press *Print card*.

**Check-in kiosk:** open *Check-in kiosk* on the entrance tablet and press *Start kiosk mode*. Members scan their card, or press **Check in with my PIN** and type their PIN on the number pad (no card needed). Five wrong PINs in a row lock PIN entry on that tablet for 5 minutes. The kiosk locks after 10 minutes without use; any staff member unlocks it with their own email and password.

**Your own card and PIN:** the Owner gives each employee a member card (Employees → **Card**). Then set your PIN under **My PIN**. At the kiosk, scanning your card just checks you in. To open the staff dashboard, tap **Check in with my PIN**, type your PIN and choose **Go to staff dashboard**.

**Record a sale:** *Record transaction* → find the member → choose Purchase, Refund, Trade / buy-in… → amount → Record. Mistakes are never edited: ask the Owner to *Void*, or record a refund.

**A member forgot their PIN:** open their profile → *Online account and PIN* → *Reset PIN* (Owner or Manager). They set a new one in the member page.

**Two people share one email:** the second person sees "Please contact a store". Open the right member's profile and press *Link online login* (after they have signed in once).

## Tiers

Each member has a tier (Base, Silver, Gold, Diamond), decided automatically on the 1st of every month from the month before (spend plus a value for each visit day). The Owner sets the rules in *Tier settings*; changes start next month. Open *Monthly update* to see that the last update worked. If a red message appears on the Dashboard, the update did not finish: the app retries every hour, and the Owner can press *Run the update now*.

To correct a tier: on the member's profile the Owner can *Set tier by hand* (with a reason) or *Recalculate* a month.

## Raffles, start to finish

1. **Raffles → New release.** Name it, tick the stores, set when it opens and closes (New York time), the number of winners and how many hours winners get to collect. It starts as a **draft**.
2. Press **Open for entries**. Members now see it in the Raffles tab of their member page and can enter between the opening and closing times. In store, open the raffle, search the member and press *Enter in raffle*.
3. A member can enter **one** store's raffle per release. Staff can remove an entry with a reason.
4. After it closes, the Owner or Manager presses **Draw winners**. This happens once and cannot be undone. You get a ranked list from #1 to the last name.
5. Press **Notify**. Winners are told by the methods the raffle allows *and* the member chose (email, member page). A winner with no match shows **Call manually**: phone them, then press *I phoned them*.
6. When a winner **collects**: press *Collected*, enter the amount and store. It is saved as a normal purchase and counts toward their tier.
7. If a winner can't collect: *Expired* or *Declined*, then **Next in line**. Then Notify again.
8. When every winner is settled, press **Finish raffle**. *Export CSV* gives the list.

## Reports and the audit log

- **Dashboard:** choose a store and dates, press *Show*.
- **Reports** (Owner/Manager): tier distribution, who moved up or down each month, sales and visits by store, raffle results.
- **Audit log** (Owner): every sensitive change, who did it and when. Filter by group, person or dates. It cannot be edited.

## Privacy requests

A member can ask for their data or to be forgotten. On their profile (Owner): **Download their data** gives a file with everything held. **Erase personal details** removes the name, phone, email, PIN and online login for good; visits and sales stay under the member number so your books add up. It cannot be undone.

## Staff accounts

Owner: *Employees → Invite* (email) or *Create* (you type a password; no email is sent). Deactivate an employee who leaves: they are signed out at once. Forgotten password: *Forgot password* on the sign-in page.

## When something looks wrong

| You see | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Email isn't set up yet" on a raffle winner | Email sending is not connected yet. Phone the winner, or connect the email sender and press *Send emails again*. |
| A member never gets a login code | Check their spam folder, ask them to request a new code after a minute, and check the email address on their profile. |
| The kiosk says "Kiosk signed out" | A staff member signs in again (the kiosk's own sign-in expired). |
| A red banner about the monthly update | Open *Monthly update*. It retries every hour. If it keeps failing, contact the developer. |
